
# TRYONIX KNOWLEDGE BASE

## About TRYONIX

TRYONIX is an AI-powered fashion e-commerce
platform with a virtual try-on feature.

Customers can explore fashion products,
view product details, and use AI-powered
virtual try-on to visualize clothing.

## Product Browsing

Customers can visit the Shop page to
browse available fashion products.

Each product has a dedicated details page.

Actual product prices and availability
must be checked from the live product database.

## AI Virtual Try-On

TRYONIX provides an AI-powered virtual
try-on feature.

Customers can upload a personal photograph
and select a supported clothing item.

The system generates a preview showing
how the selected garment may look on them.

Virtual try-on results are AI-generated
visualizations and may not perfectly represent
real-world fit, size, or fabric appearance.

## How to Use Virtual Try-On

Step 1: Open the TRYONIX website.

Step 2: Navigate to the virtual try-on page.

Step 3: Upload a clear photograph.

Step 4: Select a supported garment.

Step 5: Start the AI virtual try-on process.

Step 6: Wait for the generated preview.

The virtual try-on service may be temporarily
unavailable when the external AI inference
server is offline.

## AI Shopping Assistant

TRYONIX includes an AI shopping assistant
that supports text and voice interaction.

Customers can ask questions about outfits,
fashion recommendations, product browsing,
and the virtual try-on process.

## Important Limitations

The shopping assistant must not invent
product prices, stock availability,
shipping policies, return policies,
or order information.

Live product information should be obtained
from the TRYONIX product database.

Shipping, return, and payment policies
must be confirmed from official store
information before providing specific details.
